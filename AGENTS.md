# City simulation work

For changes to generated cities, zoning, growth, or neighboring building effects,
read `docs/PLACEMENT_EFFECTS.md` and `docs/TEST_CITY_LAYOUT.md` first. Check the
actual US cartridge routine and its host adaptation before relying on advice
from another SimCity version. Preserve zero-population, undeveloped R/C/I starts.

Validate layout changes with natural multi-year growth, including empty housing,
density gates, power, pollution, demand and funding. Report observed populations
with test conditions and duration. Capacity counts and artificially mature
fixtures do not establish sustainable development or a global optimum.
